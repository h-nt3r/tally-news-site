Ask

Questions about today's stories, answered on this computer by an open model. Nothing leaves it.

The model reads the top story's headlines. About a minute on an older computer.

How this works

Questions that are really lookups, like which stories a side missed, which ran only on one side, who reported first or what is newest, are counted from the data by code, instantly, with no model. Anything else is matched to today's stories, and you get the story's stored summary at once. Qwen3 4B writes only if you ask it to compare how the sides worded a story, or when a story has no summary yet. It answers one question at a time, remembers nothing, and your questions are not logged or sent anywhere.
